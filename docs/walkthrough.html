Comments ☆

  • We assess \(|\mathcal{A}_n^{{\mathscr I}^+}e^{i\omega_n \tau}|\) with a suitable normalisation. If \(\mathcal{A}_n^{{\mathscr I}^+}\) is not numerically stable, then we are making (good looking) plots that change for each grid resolution.
  • We need call “relative modal weight” the quantity \[ W_n(\tau)=\frac{\left\vert\mathcal{A}_n^{{\mathscr I}^+} e^{i\omega_n\tau}\right\vert}{\max_n \left\vert\mathcal{A}_n^{{\mathscr I}^+}e^{i\omega_n\tau}\right\vert},\]

In order to take into account the beating between eigenvalues of opposite real part, it is natural to evaluate \[\sum_{\substack{\mathrm{Re}(\omega_n)\geq 0 \\ \mathrm{Im}(\omega_n)\geq 0}}\frac{\mathcal{A}_n^{{\mathscr I}^+}e^{i\omega_n\tau}+c.c.}{2}\]

We will consider \[ W_n^{c.c.}(\tau)=\frac{\left\vert\frac{\mathcal{A}_n^{{\mathscr I}^+} e^{i\omega_n\tau}+c.c.}{2}\right\vert}{\max_n \left\vert\frac{\mathcal{A}_n^{{\mathscr I}^+} e^{i\omega_n\tau}+c.c.}{2}\right\vert},\]

\[ W_n^{c.c.,n.r.}(\tau)=\left\vert\frac{\mathcal{A}_n^{{\mathscr I}^+} e^{i\omega_n\tau}+c.c.}{2}\right\vert,\]
